Cube Swipe

A relaxing cube puzzle

  • Puzzle
  • Free
  • iPhone · Web
  • English · Español
Play on the web Coming soon on theApp Store

Flat tiles in three colors create the illusion of a cube. Swipe around a point to turn a group of three, and rebuild the cube: yellow on top, red on the left, blue on the right. Easy to learn, and every move changes the whole picture.

50 levelsFrom 2×2×2 to 4×4×4, each one a little harder.
A secret levelAn extreme 8×8×8 cube waits at the very end.
Hints you keepStuck? A hint shows exactly where to swipe, and unused hints are saved.
Free playEasy, Medium or Hard shuffles, whenever you want.
Build your ownPaint the tiles and create your own challenge.
Calm by designSoft sounds, gentle haptics, and light and dark themes.

Inspired by the Propp Lozenge, the magnetic puzzle invented by James Propp and designed by Oskar van Deventer.
