Cube Swipe

Out now

Swipe to turn the tiles and rebuild the cube.

Puzzle · Free · iPhone · Web

Play
  1. 1Swipe around a dot to turn the three tiles that touch it.
  2. 2Rebuild the cube: yellow on top, red on the left, blue on the right.
  3. 3Finish in fewer moves for more stars. Stuck? A hint shows the way.

Inspired by the Propp Lozenge, the magnetic puzzle invented by James Propp and designed by Oskar van Deventer.
