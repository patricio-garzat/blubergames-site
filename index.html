Cube Swipe

A magnetic puzzle, rebuilt for your phone.

Flat tiles in three colors create the illusion of a cube. Turn groups of three tiles to rebuild it: yellow on top, red on the left and blue on the right. It's easy to learn, but every move changes the whole picture.

  • 50 levels, from 2×2×2 to 4×4×4, each harder than the last
  • A secret extreme level on an 8×8×8 cube
  • Stars for every level and hints you can save
  • Free play in Easy, Medium and Hard
  • Build your own shuffle and play from there
  • In English and Spanish, with light and dark themes
  • iPhone · iPad · web
  • Swipe controls
  • Free to play
Play now App Store: coming soon

Based on the Propp Lozenge magnetic puzzle by James Propp and Oskar van Deventer.
